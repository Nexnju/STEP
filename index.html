01 The Program

Where literacy sits at 20%, a weekend with a mentor changes the whole road ahead. STEP trains local youth to teach, guide and lead.

Turkana County is an Arid and Semi-Arid Land in north-western Kenya, where extreme poverty, drought and child marriage keep children out of the classroom. STEP youth work with local schools, running after-school activities on weekends and school holidays to lift learning outcomes.

Through education, mentorship and collaboration, we empower communities, bridge inequities and create a brighter future for all.

01

Another Kind of Christmas

Every December, the Strathmore Christmas Caravan collects and distributes food, school supplies and clothing to some of Turkana's most vulnerable families.

02

Revision Camps

Exam preparation for seniors with career guidance, mentorship and scholarship-application support plus numeracy and literacy for ECE children.

03

Women Enterprise

Skills training, business basics and life skills for women and out-of-school girls. Beadwork gives work and purpose; peer counselling and story-telling give support.

04

Sport Management & Leadership

Workshops for managers, coaches and captains across Turkana County, using sport to build values in the youth of Lodwar.

05

Youth Mentorship

Character building that grows resilience and personal values, with honest conversations on leadership and peer influence.

06

Substance Abuse Workshops

Open, supportive sessions that help young people understand the risks and choose a different path.

05 Our Story

Jan 2023

Christina (Strathmore University, Nairobi) and Raquel (Munich) meet through a friend, sharing a dream: young people from around the world building a better future for women and communities on the margins.

Dec 2023

The first international "Another Kind of Christmas" educational camp opens in Turkana.

Leadership events begin in Munich, Nairobi and online. 70+ volunteers from five continents have since joined us.
